About The Trust

A Mission Driven By Compassion & Action

Anagha Educational & Welfare Trust is a non-profit organization dedicated to uplifting underserved communities through education, healthcare, women empowerment, and environmental sustainability. For over a decade, we have worked at the grassroots to create lasting change.

Our Mission

To provide quality education, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities to marginalized communities, empowering them to build self-reliant futures.

Our Vision

A society where every individual has access to education, health, dignity, and the opportunity to thrive regardless of their background.

Core Values

Compassion, integrity, inclusivity, and sustainability guide every decision we make and every life we touch.
